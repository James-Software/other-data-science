01About

Curiosity required.
Experience optional.

Acalanes Data Fest is a student-run hackathon hosted by the Data Science Club. Pick a dataset, ask a good question, and build an answer with a team. Whether it’s your first chart or your fiftieth model, there’s a place for you here.

02Format

Three steps. That’s it.

01

Explore

Form a team, pick a problem, and dig into a dataset. Ask questions until you find a good one.

02

Build

Analyze, design, and iterate. Mentors and peers are around when you get stuck.

03

Share

Demo what you made and share what you learned with the Acalanes community.

04FAQ

Good questions.

Who can join?

Any Acalanes student. No tryouts, no prerequisites.

Do I need to know data science?

No. The event is beginner-friendly — you’ll learn as you build.

Do I need a team beforehand?

No. Come solo or bring friends — you’ll form teams at the event.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It’s run by students, for students.

When does registration open?

Not yet. Follow @aca.datasci.club and you’ll hear it first.
