Questions

Who can take part?

Acalanes Data Fest is for Acalanes students. If you go to Acalanes, you’re in.

Do I need experience with data?

No. Beginners are welcome — curiosity matters more than experience, and mentors and peers will be around to help.

How do teams work?

Come with a team or find one when you arrive. Teams form around the questions people want to explore.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It’s free for all Acalanes students.

When does registration open?

Not yet — the date and details are still being confirmed. Follow @aca.datasci.club on Instagram and you’ll hear it first.
